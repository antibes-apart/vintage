Beautiful vintage Doufeu cocotte in enameled cast iron by Cousances, the prestigious high-end heritage line of Le Creuset, made in France. A large-format and highly functional piece, combining traditional craftsmanship with innovative French culinary design. This model is known for its unique condensation lid system: the hollow lid is designed to retain moisture during cooking. It can also hold ice cubes to create a thermal shock, encouraging continuous condensation. This process naturally bastes the food, enhancing tenderness and intensifying flavors. The enameled cast iron ensures gentle, even, and energy-efficient cooking, preserving the natural taste and texture of ingredients. A method widely appreciated by professional chefs for its ability to produce melt-in-the-mouth results. Fully compatible with all heat sources, including induction, this cocotte is both practical and highly durable. Its large size makes it ideal for family meals and slow-cooked dishes.
