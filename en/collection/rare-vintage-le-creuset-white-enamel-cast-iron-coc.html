Rare Vintage Le Creuset White Enamel Cast Iron Cocotte with Floral Pattern

Beautiful vintage Le Creuset enamel cast iron cocotte in white with a charming retro floral design in blue and grey tones. A rare and collectible older model, perfect both for cooking and for displaying in a vintage or country-style kitchen. High-quality enameled cast iron, ideal for slow cooking, stews, soups, casseroles, and everyday use. The interior is clean and well preserved for its age. Approximate dimensions: diameter: ~26 cm height without lid: ~10 cm Good vintage condition with normal signs of age and some enamel wear/chips around the edges of the lid and pot (see photos). These details add to its authentic vintage character. A rare decorative Le Creuset piece that is becoming increasingly difficult to find in this floral pattern.
