Le Creuset / Cousances – Cast Iron Casserole Dish – Model 16 – Vintage – Black

Beautiful large vintage casserole by Cousances, the prestigious high-end heritage brand later absorbed by Le Creuset. Model 16, crafted in heavy-duty cast iron, finished in elegant black enamel. A classic and timeless piece of French cookware, combining durability, functionality, and historical value. This casserole offers excellent heat retention and even cooking, making it ideal for slow-cooked dishes and traditional recipes. This item is in vintage condition with signs of use consistent with age. Please see photos, which are part of the description and should be carefully reviewed. Cousances pieces were discontinued in the 1980s after the acquisition by Le Creuset in 1957, making them increasingly rare and highly sought after by collectors and cooking enthusiasts. They represent an important part of French culinary heritage and craftsmanship.
