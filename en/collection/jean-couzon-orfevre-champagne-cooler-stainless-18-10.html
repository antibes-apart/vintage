A French champagne cooler conceived as table service rather than as a promotional object: there is no house branding, only the maker's signature 'Jean Couzon Orfèvre' and the 'INOX 18/10' steel specification beneath the base. The tulip-shaped body flares into a generous rolled rim and rests on a stepped pedestal foot finished with a beaded border and a ribbed band — the vocabulary of classic French orfèvrerie translated into stainless steel. The two ring handles are fully articulated, hung from baluster mounts with medallion-capped knops, and their rings carry fine gadrooned decoration. The mirror-polished exterior contrasts with the brushed interior. Following our guide to French champagne buckets, we describe it simply as a later-20th-century Jean Couzon cooler, without attaching an unsupported model name or year.
