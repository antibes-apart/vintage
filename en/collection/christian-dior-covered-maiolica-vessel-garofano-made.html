A covered ceramic vessel bearing the name of the French house of Christian Dior, yet explicitly marked as made in Italy — a reminder that French design and French manufacture are not the same claim. The pale bluish-white tin glaze is painted in the Faenza "Garofano" (carnation) manner: stylised carnation blooms with sweeping green foliage in orange-red, cobalt, olive and yellow, above the characteristic band of blue scale-work waves. The fitted, gently scalloped lid repeats the palette with a hatched rust-red lattice border and scattered flower sprays, and is crowned by a looped finial echoing the two scrolled handles. The Garofano decoration is a classic of Faenza maiolica; the mark does not name a pottery, and we do not attribute the piece to one.
