Designed in 1958 by Raymond Loewy for Le Creuset, La Coquelle brought the streamlined language of post-war industrial design to French enamelled cast iron. Its softly squared body, winged handles and sculpted lid with recessed oval grip make it one of the defining mid-century pieces of French cookware — here in a soft jade-green enamel, as much at home on the table as on display.
