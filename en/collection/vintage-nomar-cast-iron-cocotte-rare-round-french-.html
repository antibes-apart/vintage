Vintage Nomar Cast Iron Cocotte – Rare Round French Dutch Oven – Staub Group – 1970s Floral Enamel

Rare vintage Nomar cast iron cocotte, made in France and part of the Staub group. Beautiful round model in warm mustard yellow enamel with stunning retro floral folk decoration typical of the 1970s French cookware style. Heavy quality enameled cast iron with white enamel interior and original lid. Perfect for slow cooking, stews, roasting or as a decorative farmhouse kitchen piece. Approximate dimensions: 24 cm diameter without handles. Vintage condition with light signs of use and small enamel marks on the edges consistent with age. Interior remains in very nice condition overall. A rare and highly collectible French cast iron piece for lovers of vintage cookware, Nomar, Staub, Cousances and Le Creuset style items.
