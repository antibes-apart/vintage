A covered oval dresser or trinket box from the Limoges house of Limoges Castel, in the classic cobalt-and-gold manner associated with French presentation porcelain. The deep, almost midnight-blue ground is worked with dense gilt scrollwork around the sides, while the domed lid frames a white reserve with a printed polychrome galant scene — a courting couple in a pastoral landscape — within a raised gilt rocaille cartouche. The interior is glazed in the same dark blue, and the lid seats onto a white, gilt-edged rim. It comes with its original blue presentation carton, whose printed shield matches the gold mark on the porcelain itself — a small but useful piece of evidence tying the object to the house.

Maker
Limoges Castel
Origin
Limoges, France
Materials
Porcelain, cobalt-blue ground, gilt decoration, printed polychrome scene
Dimensions
Length approx. 14 cm; height of base approx. 4.5 cm (without lid)
Marks & signatures
Gold shield device on the base reading 'LIMOGES CASTEL® · FRANCE', with '22 K GOLD' beneath. Original blue printed carton reading 'PORCELAINE LIMOGES CASTEL FRANCE' around the same shield
Condition
Very good condition. Glossy glaze; gilding bright with no notable rubbing; no chips or cracks observed. Presentation carton shows light wear to the edges (see photographs).
