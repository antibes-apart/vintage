Rare Vintage Le Creuset Raymond Loewy Coquelle Nº45 with Matching Plate & Lid

140€

Rare and collectible vintage Le Creuset “Coquelle” cocotte designed by Raymond Loewy, sold together with its original lid and matching serving plate. This iconic mid-century model features the elegant sculptural shape typical of the Raymond Loewy designs, in a beautiful pastel yellow color with contrasting black handles. The underside is marked 45, corresponding to the large Coquelle model. Made in enameled cast iron, this piece is both functional and highly decorative, perfect for collectors of vintage Le Creuset or mid-century kitchenware. The set is in good vintage condition overall, with signs of age and use visible on the edges, handles and underside, as shown in the photos. The interior enamel remains nice overall.
