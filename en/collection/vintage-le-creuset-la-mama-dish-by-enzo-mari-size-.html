Vintage Le Creuset “La Mama” Dish by Enzo Mari – Size 29 – Made in France

95€

Rare and highly collectible vintage Le Creuset serving / roasting dish from the iconic “La Mama” collection designed by renowned Italian designer Enzo Mari in the 1970s. This beautiful oval enameled cast iron piece features the famous sculptural side handles characteristic of the La Mama collection, with a vibrant enamel exterior and light cream enamel interior. A true example of functional mid-century design, blending cookware and decorative art. Perfect for gratins, roasted vegetables, lasagna, oven dishes or elegant table presentation. The interior enamel is in very good vintage condition with light traces of use. The exterior shows some enamel wear and small chips around the rim consistent with age and use, without affecting functionality.
