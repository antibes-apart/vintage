A presentation plate from the Limoges house of Limoges Castel, complete with the paperwork that so rarely survives. The wide border is laid in a deep carmine-red ground, richly worked with gilt floral sprays and finished with a gilt rim line; the white well carries a printed polychrome galant scene of a young man serenading a seated lady with a guitar in a garden setting, in the eighteenth-century fête galante manner. The name printed within the scene records the composition the decoration was sold as being after — it is part of the transfer, not a signature. It comes with its original blue presentation box, the house's bilingual certificate explaining the 'Limoges Castel' backstamp, a printed house leaflet and a brass hanger, making it an unusually well-documented example of French presentation porcelain.

Very good condition. Bright red ground and gilding with no notable wear; no chips or cracks observed. Box shows light wear to the edges; certificate creased from folding (see photographs).
