Who is responsible

Browsing the site

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which receives your IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and keep them secure. Fonts are served from this site; no third-party font service is used.

Contacting us and buying

When you write to us by email, WhatsApp or Messenger, we use your contact details and messages to answer you and, if you buy, to carry out the sale (performance of a contract). WhatsApp and Messenger are operated by Meta under their own privacy policies. Sales records are kept as long as French accounting law requires (10 years); other messages are deleted when they are no longer needed.

The identification agent

When you use the identification agent, we store the photos and notes you submit, the agent's answer, a case reference and a random browser identifier saved in your browser's local storage (to apply the daily limit). Your IP address is used only in hashed form for anti-abuse limits. Location and other metadata are removed from the photos before storage. Please photograph only the object, not people or personal documents.

Purposes: to give you an answer and to improve the agent, by reviewing past cases, correcting how markings are read and enriching our reference archive. Cases are never published and never become documentary evidence on their own.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing and improving the agent (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). Storing the case is a condition of use; you can object and ask for deletion at any time, as explained below.

Recipients: Cloudflare (hosting of the agent, database and private photo storage) and Google (Gemini, which reads the photos to extract their markings; under Google's data processing terms, it does not use them to train its models). Both are US companies; transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Retention: cases (photos, notes and answers) are deleted automatically 5 years after submission, or earlier on request. Daily usage counters are deleted after 2 days. The browser identifier stays in your browser until you clear its site data.

The identification agent is reserved for people aged 18 or over.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy of it. For the identification agent, email vintagetreasurecontact@gmail.com with your case reference: we delete that case and every case sent from the same browser. We answer within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), the French data protection authority.
